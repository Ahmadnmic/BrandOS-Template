# Odense Basket, the brand as law

Everything visual flows from brand/tokens.json (DTCG; --sys-* roles are
the only legal styling values). Generated ladders live in
brand/ladders.json; the contrast contract is brand/contrast-matrix.json;
the termbank is brand/terms.json; every usage rule is in
brand/rules.json (both languages).

Never invent brand facts, colors, imagery or logo artwork. When a fact
is not in these files or the portal, ask.

The brand MCP (npm run mcp) exposes this as executable tools:
check_contrast, check_copy, get_token, get_rules and more. Prefer the
tools over reading files when they answer the question.

Rules in force: 30 usage rules across 5 chapters. Writing: no em or en dashes as pause marks, no AI-tell phrases; the brand's languages are in brand.config.ts langs.
